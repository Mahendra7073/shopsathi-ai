# Future Scope & Roadmap

## 🚀 Post-Hackathon Roadmap

ShopSathi AI is built on an extensible foundation designed for enterprise scalability:

### 1. Multi-Modal Vision Inspection
* Allow customers to upload photos or short videos of damaged packages directly within the Chat Agent.
* Use Gemini Vision capabilities to inspect tears, defects, or incorrect items and trigger instant automated return approvals.

### 2. Multi-Channel Messaging Integrations
* Deploy ShopSathi as an official **WhatsApp Business** and **Telegram** bot.
* Send proactive delivery tracking updates and return pickup OTP notifications.

### 3. Live WebRTC Human Handoff
* Enable seamless browser-to-browser voice transfer from the AI Voice Agent to a live Tier 2 human agent with full live transcription history.

### 4. Predictive Order Retention & Discount Offers
* When a customer requests cancellation, dynamically offer personalized discounts or alternate sizes to retain the order while respecting customer intent.
