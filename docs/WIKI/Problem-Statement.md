# Problem Statement

## 📉 The Fragmented Support Dilemma in E-Commerce

Traditional e-commerce platforms suffer from severe operational friction in their customer support workflows:

### 1. Disjointed Customer Journeys
Customers must jump between separate interfaces:
- Product catalogs to browse items.
- Order history portals to check parcel tracking.
- Complicated return wizards to check policy eligibility.
- Detached contact forms or phone queues to ask simple questions.

### 2. The Failure of Scripted Chatbots
Most existing e-commerce chatbots are rigid, rule-based keyword matchers or ungrounded LLMs:
- **No Action Capability**: They cannot perform actions like cancelling an order, validating a delivery date, or initiating a refund payout.
- **Hallucinated Answers**: Generative models without strict tool integrations invent fake order numbers, incorrect policy terms, or fabricated refund dates.
- **Dead-End Frustration**: When a customer query falls outside canned FAQs, bots fail without transferring context to a human agent.

### 3. Language & Accessibility Barriers
India's diverse e-commerce market contains millions of vernacular shoppers who communicate naturally in **Hindi** or **Hinglish**, or prefer **Voice Calls** over typing long text descriptions. Traditional platforms fail to support natural conversational speech.
