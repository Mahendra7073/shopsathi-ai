# Problem Statement

## 📉 The Fragmented Support Dilemma in E-Commerce

Traditional e-commerce platforms suffer from severe operational friction in their customer support workflows:

### 1. Disjointed Customer Journeys
Customers are forced to jump between disjointed interfaces:
- Product catalogs to browse items.
- Order history portals to check parcel tracking.
- Complicated return wizards to check policy eligibility.
- Detached contact forms or long phone queues to resolve simple issues.

### 2. The Failure of Traditional Scripted Chatbots
Most existing e-commerce chatbots are rigid FAQ-only bots or ungrounded generative widgets:
- **No Transactional Action Capability**: They cannot perform concrete actions such as cancelling an order, validating a delivery date, checking return eligibility, or initiating a refund payout.
- **Hallucinated or Speculative Answers**: Generative models without strict backend API grounding risk inventing fake order numbers, incorrect policy terms, or fabricated refund schedules.
- **Dead-End Frustration**: When a customer query falls outside canned FAQs, bots fail without transferring context to a human representative.

### 3. Language & Accessibility Barriers
India's diverse e-commerce market contains millions of vernacular shoppers who communicate naturally in **Hindi** or **Hinglish**, or prefer **Voice Interactions** over typing long text queries. Traditional platforms fail to support natural conversational speech.
