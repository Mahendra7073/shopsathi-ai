# Project Overview

## 📌 What is ShopSathi AI?
**ShopSathi AI** is an intelligent, multi-modal customer support and shopping assistant designed for e-commerce platforms. The name *"ShopSathi"* translates to *"Shopping Companion"*, embodying our mission to assist customers through their entire shopping lifecycle—from initial product discovery to complex post-purchase fulfillment.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience
1. **Online Shoppers**: Seeking instant, 24/7 resolution for order status, cancellations, returns, and refunds without waiting on long support hold queues.
2. **E-Commerce Merchants & Brands**: Looking to reduce customer service operational costs, automate up to 85% of routine Tier-1 inquiries, and eliminate dead-end bot frustrations.
3. **Customer Support Teams**: Gaining clean, pre-triaged support tickets with full conversational history when escalation to Tier-2 human agents is required.

---

## 🔑 Core Value Propositions
* **Zero Hallucinations**: Grounded directly in live database state via deterministic REST APIs.
* **Multi-Modal Accessibility**: Seamless switching between text chat (English, Hindi, Hinglish) and real-time natural voice (`+91 8031339824`).
* **Deterministic Policy Enforcement**: Enforces genuine business rules (e.g., 7-day return policy window, hygiene restrictions, state-based cancellation blocks).
* **Intelligent Human Escalation**: Automatically recognizes unresolvable friction (e.g., failed UPI gateway confirmations) and transitions issues to human supervisors.
